## US / Canada / hosting issues

- [Streamlit Community Cloud terms](https://streamlit.io/terms-of-use) restrict content requiring heightened legal protection, including financial information. Fictitious calculator values and public research are product boundaries, not confirmation from the host that this project is eligible. Host confirmation is an unresolved release gate.
- [Streamlit security documentation](https://docs.streamlit.io/deploy/streamlit-community-cloud/get-started/trust-and-security) describes inherited GitHub permissions. Explicit Sharing viewers are only part of access review. [Cloud limitations](https://docs.streamlit.io/deploy/streamlit-community-cloud/status) also require attention to hosting/telemetry differences.
- [SEC adviser overview](https://www.sec.gov/files/divisions/investment/iaregulation/memoia.htm) discusses compensation, business activity and securities advice. [Canadian regulator registration guidance](https://www.asc.ca/Registrant-and-Market-Regulation/Registrant-Toolkit/Getting-Registered) describes the business trigger and jurisdictional registration/exemptions. These are starting points, not a legal conclusion that a private unpaid app is exempt. Personalized recommendations, subscriptions, affiliate compensation, public promotion or trading links require fresh counsel review before introduction.
- [Canadian privacy regulator overview](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/pipeda_brief/) distinguishes commercial handling and strictly personal purposes. Do not assume a two-user app qualifies for an exclusion or resolves provincial requirements. Confirm operator/user jurisdictions, host location, logs, retention and transfer obligations with qualified advice when needed.

## Integration admission rule

Before any backend source is enabled, record: exact endpoints/dataset and owner; applicable dated terms/contract; named authorized users and geography; storage, redistribution and derivative analytics rights; attribution; quotas/caching/deletion; fees; timestamp/currency/units/corporate actions/restatements; missing-data behavior; privacy; regression and load tests. Ambiguity means link/display-only or unavailable, never an environment attestation masquerading as permission.
